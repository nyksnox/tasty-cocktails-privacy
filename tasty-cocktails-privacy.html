Privacy Policy — Tasty Cocktails

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Tasty Cocktails (`com.elifselina.tastycocktails`) is a casual merge puzzle game developed by Elif Selin Aksoy Yeltekin ("Nyksnox").

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • The game runs entirely offline on your device.
  • No user accounts, sign-ins, or registrations are required.
  • No analytics, advertising SDKs, trackers, or third-party data collectors are included in the app.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or device identifiers.

2. Children's Privacy

Because Tasty Cocktails does not collect any personal information from any user, no data is collected from children.

3. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the game introduces new features that affect privacy, this policy will be updated accordingly before release.
